Reading the output

The model has exactly two outputs and no notion of where — it answers "is there a person in this frame", not "where is the person". A positive result on a real flight is useful because the drone knows its own position when the frame is captured, so a hit plus telemetry is a coordinate.

Try the right half of each frame. On the frame containing a person, cropping to the right removes the heat signature and the model correctly reports nothing. On the empty frame, the right half contains a warm corner, and the model calls it a person. That second one is a false positive, and it is the most honest thing on this page: the model reacts to warm regions, which means some of its accuracy is probably temperature and colour statistics rather than a learned sense of what a person's shape is.

Caveats worth stating

  • Only two frames survive from the original dataset, so this is a demonstration of the model, not an evaluation of it.
  • The jet colormap rescales to each frame's own temperature range, so colours are not comparable between frames.
  • Weights are float16. uint8 quantisation was tried first and was rejected — it inverted the empty frame's prediction while still looking confident.
  • Class order (0 = person) is not stored in the model file. It was recovered by testing, and the method is written up in the repository.
