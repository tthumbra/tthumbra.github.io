About

I'm a computer science student at Purdue, junior standing by credits. Most of what I build ends up on the boundary between software and something physical — a car, a drone, a sensor feed that doesn't behave the way the spec said it would.

Right now that means drivetrain physics models for Purdue Electric Racing, written in MATLAB, so the team can find out how a car will behave before anyone machines the part. Before that: an autonomous search-and-rescue drone that ran a 2 MB thermal classifier onboard because the payload budget was 150 grams, and an NLP classifier for finding clickbait in YouTube transcripts.

Before Purdue I was in San Jose. I taught algebra through Calculus II and Java to about seventy students, coached Taekwondo for a couple hundred hours, and competed in USACO through Silver division.

I'm looking for software engineering and ML engineering internships. The fastest way to reach me is email.

Drivetrain Physics Simulator

Sep 2025 – present

Purdue Electric Racing

MATLAB models of EV drivetrain dynamics, built so the team can predict performance numbers without burning a testing cycle to find them. Steering and throttle response are derived from historical test data, which sharpened fidelity most where it mattered: corner entry and acceleration.

MATLAB · vehicle dynamics

Autonomous Drone Search & Rescue

Feb 2024 – Aug 2024

Independent

An end-to-end drone platform for locating missing persons in thermal imagery. A MobileNetV2 classifier runs onboard rather than round-tripping to a ground station — squeezed from 1.41 GB to 4.5 MB across four trials to fit a 150 gram payload budget. I wrote the full stack — flight path planning, sensor fusion — and modeled the enclosure in CAD.

Python · Java · TensorFlow · MobileNetV2 · CAD

Clickbait Detection Model

Jan 2023 – Jul 2023

Inspirit AI

A CNN-based NLP classifier that reads YouTube transcripts and decides whether the title was lying to you. Tokenization and feature extraction pipeline, hyperparameter tuning, validated on precision and recall rather than raw accuracy — the classes are lopsided enough that accuracy would have flattered the model. Presented to 200+ people at an online demo.
