About

I'm a computer science student at Purdue, junior standing by credits. Most of what I build sits between software and hardware — vehicle dynamics models, drone payloads, sensor pipelines.

Right now that means drivetrain physics models for Purdue Electric Racing, written in MATLAB, so the team can predict how a car will behave before the parts are machined. Before that: an autonomous search-and-rescue drone that ran a 4.5 MB thermal classifier onboard because the payload budget was 150 grams, and an NLP classifier for finding clickbait in YouTube transcripts.

Before Purdue I was in San Jose. I taught algebra through Calculus II and Java to about seventy students, coached Taekwondo for a couple hundred hours, and competed in USACO through Silver division.

I'm looking for software engineering and ML engineering internships. The fastest way to reach me is email.

Purdue Electric Racing

MATLAB models of EV drivetrain dynamics, built so the team can predict performance numbers without spending a testing cycle to find them. Steering and throttle response are derived from historical test data, which improved fidelity most at corner entry and acceleration.

Autonomous Drone Search & Rescue

Feb 2024 – Aug 2024

Independent

An end-to-end drone platform for locating missing persons in thermal imagery. A MobileNetV2 classifier runs onboard rather than round-tripping to a ground station — reduced from 1.41 GB to 4.5 MB across four trials to fit a 150 gram payload budget. I wrote the full stack — flight path planning, sensor fusion — and modeled the enclosure in CAD.
